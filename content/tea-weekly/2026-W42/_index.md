## Deals

Checked this week (all seen on 2026-10-12, directly on each shop's own site).

This is the quietest deal week we have logged. Across eight shops and roughly 1,400 listings, the number of teas whose price moved since last Monday is **zero**. Yunnan Sourcing's nine markdowns are the same nine, at the same prices. Tea Drunk's one markdown is the same one, still sold out. Red Blossom and White2Tea are at zero discounted items for the sixth straight week.

That is not a failure of the week — it is what the calendar does. The autumn restock is still arriving, and shops do not discount inventory they just paid for. The useful reading this week is in what is *landing*, not what is falling.

### Seven Cups — a Weekend Specials shelf, but no markdowns on it

Seven Cups is featuring a six-tea **Weekend Specials** selection, and all six are aged shu pu-erh — an unusual bit of curation in a season when everyone else is pushing fresh harvest:

| [Run Lin (Rainy Day) 2005](https://sevencups.com/shop/run-lin-rainy-day-shu-puer-cake-2005/) | 400 g shu cake | $25.00–$240.00 |
| [Tai Lian Fangzhuan 2004](https://sevencups.com/shop/2004-tai-lian-shu-puer-brick-250g/) | 250 g shu brick | $119.00 |
| [Gongting (Palace Puer) 2012](https://sevencups.com/shop/gong-ting-palace-puer-2012/) | loose leaf | $23.00–$226.75 |

Two caveats, and they matter. First, "special" here means featured, not reduced — none of the six carries a struck-through price, so there is no discount to claim. Second, **three of the six are already out of stock**: Run Lin 2005, Tai Lian 2004, and Gongting 2012. The three you can actually buy are the Ma Guo Tou, the Jinggu brick, and the Jia Cang.

Of those, the [Jinggu brick at $26](https://sevencups.com/shop/2013-jing-gu-xiao-fang-zhuan-shu-puer-brick-100g/) is the one we would point a newcomer at. A 100 g brick of thirteen-year-old shu is a low-stakes way to find out whether aged ripe pu-erh is for you — small enough that a miss costs less than a restaurant lunch, old enough to have shed the damp "wo dui" pile-ferment note that puts people off young shu.

One honesty note: Seven Cups' category pages returned identical content for three different category URLs when we checked them this morning, so we could not verify the full contents of their New 2026 Teas listing. The six teas above are what the site actually served, and each was confirmed on its own product page. If you are hunting their fresh 2026 oolongs, browse the site by hand this week.

## Events

Two notes before the list. The October 10 Chocolate & Tea Pairing has now passed. And there is one genuinely new item this week — the first *online* course with fixed dates we have been able to list.

Tea-adjacent rather than a tasting, but it is the cheapest way to spend three hours in that studio.
