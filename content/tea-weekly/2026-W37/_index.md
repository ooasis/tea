### Worth the drive

- **[Pennsylvania Tea Festival](https://www.teafestpa.com/)** — Friday–Saturday, September 18–19, 2026, The Rosemary House & Sweet Remembrances Tea Room, 120 S Market St, Mechanicsburg, PA. Roughly 24 vendors, tastings, lectures, and garden tours. Tickets are on sale now; the [festival listing](https://www.festivalsinpa.com/festival/332/pennsylvania-tea-festival) quotes $15 advance / $18 at the gate — confirm on the festival's own ticket page before you go. About three hours from NYC, and the closest full tea festival to the metro area this season.
