## Events

### This coming weekend

- **[Pennsylvania Tea Festival](https://www.teafestpa.com/home)** — Friday–Saturday, September 18–19, 2026, The Rosemary House & Sweet Remembrances Tea Room, 120 S Market St, Mechanicsburg, PA. This is the one to plan around: guided tastings from multiple tea experts, roughly two dozen vendors, informative programs, musical entertainment, garden activities, and a handcrafted tea cozy competition you can vote in. Runs rain or shine. Confirm ticket prices on the festival's own [tickets page](https://www.teafestpa.com/home) before you drive — about three hours from NYC, and the closest full tea festival to the metro area this season.
