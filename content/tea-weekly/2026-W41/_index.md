Two changes worth noting against last week. The Feng Qing "Classic 58" Dian Hong, discounted for four straight weeks, has dropped out of the collection entirely. And the Purple Tea Sampler that sold out in September is back as the Purple Black Tea Sampler — in a Spring 2026 pressing, at full price.

**Is last year's black tea worth buying?** Usually yes, and more so than for green tea. Chinese black tea (红茶) is fully oxidized and does not depend on the fresh, grassy top notes that make a year-old Longjing a disappointment. A Spring 2025 Jin Jun Mei stored sealed and cool has lost very little; the Spring 2023 Yixing Hong at $5 for 25 g is a cheap way to taste a regional style you probably haven't tried. If you want the freshest expression of a tea you already love, pay full price for the 2026. If you are exploring, the 2025 shelf is the better value this week.

The standing offers still stack: [free gifts and BOGO](https://yunnansourcing.us/pages/free-and-bogo) in five tiers starting at $45, and [free US shipping on $75+](https://yunnansourcing.us/pages/free-shipping-when-you-spend-75-or-more). Same fine print as always — the BOGO item must be added to the cart manually for the code to work, and the $75 shipping threshold is measured *after* any discount code is applied.

### Tea Drunk — a markdown, and it is already gone

After weeks of no discounts at all across its 105 listings, [Tea Drunk](https://www.teadrunk.com/products/yan-cha-da-hong-pao-great-red-robe) marked down its Spring 2019 [Da Hong Pao (Great Red Robe) 大紅袍](https://www.teadrunk.com/products/yan-cha-da-hong-pao-great-red-robe) — $68.00 → $55.00 for the 32 g box, $29.00 → $23.00 for the 8 g packet, across both the Spring 2019 and Spring 2019 II lots.

The reason given on the page is refreshingly blunt: *"The items are discounted because the labels are discolored."* That is the best kind of discount — nothing wrong with the tea. Unfortunately all four variants show **sold out** as of this morning, so this is a watch-list item rather than a buy. Tea Drunk's Wuyi yancha rarely goes on sale at any price, so if a cosmetic-defect markdown reappears, it is worth moving on.

| [Naka Sun-Dried](https://sevencups.com/shop/naka-sun-dried-yunnan-black-tea/) | Naka Village, Mengsong Township, Menghai County, Xishuangbanna | $18.50–$36.00 (50 g / 100 g) |

No discount on any of them, but the two sun-dried Yunnan blacks are the interesting pair. Both Naka and Youle are village names better known to pu-erh drinkers than to black-tea drinkers — the same famous gardens, processed as 红茶 instead of being pressed into cakes. Buying both and tasting them side by side is a terroir comparison with the processing variable held constant, which is an unusually clean experiment for $41.50.
